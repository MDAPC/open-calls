    How this works

    Read, then checked

    Every week a script reads the sites marked Checked weekly under Resources. Each new item is then opened and checked: is it a real open call, when does it close, who can apply, and what is the organisation's own page. News stories, past winners and closed calls are left out. Where a page does not say something, the card says "not stated" rather than guessing.

    Some calls first reach us through Hostwriter's members' forum, HostWIRE. We list one only when the organisation's own public page states it, and the card links to that page. Nothing from the forum itself is published.

    Always confirm before you apply

    Deadlines and rules change. Treat each card as a pointer and read the call on the organisation's own page before applying. Times zones on deadlines are rarely stated: aim for a day early.

    Never pay to apply

    Genuine grants and fellowships do not charge application fees. If a listing here asks you for money, do not pay, and tell us so we can remove it.

    Privacy

    No accounts, no tracking, no cookies. The calls you save stay in your own browser.

    Help

    Spotted a mistake, a closed call, or a source we should read? Write to us. The data is free to reuse: opportunities.json and the RSS feed update with every edition.
